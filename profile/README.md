# ImageGlass - View Pictures Without Saving Over Them

ImageGlass is a viewer for a folder of images you may open. Frames of an animation, a slideshow, and a checkerboard behind transparency are ways of looking. They are not a retouch.

---

## Product Snapshot

You open a directory, see one picture large, and move to the next. Fit-to-window shows the whole frame. Actual size shows the pixels. A transparent PNG can sit on a checkerboard so you can tell empty from white. None of that has to write the file.

The risk is a save or a paste back onto the same name. Read the dialog. If you only meant to look, close without saving and check the time in Explorer. A folder you do not own is a folder to close, not a folder to convert.

## Live Production

Use it to review a shoot or a scan before an editor. Step once, zoom once, and decide what you will crop later. Do not delete from a card you have not copied. Do not learn a delete key on the only copy of a job.

If you need a smaller JPEG for a page, export or save a copy beside the original, then look at the copy. ImageGlass does not remove a watermark you did not make, and it does not include a stock library.

---

## Pixels, Not the Fit

Fit-to-window shrinks a large photo so the whole frame is visible. That view can look soft even when the file is sharp. Switch to actual size on one frame before you reshoot or re-export. An animation frame is the same idea: pause on one frame and look, instead of judging the blur of playback.

---

## Setup Path

Prerequisites: A folder of images you may view, opened from the real directory.

1. Install ImageGlass and open that folder.
2. Open one photo and switch between fit and actual size.
3. Step to the next file and back.
4. Close without saving.
5. In Explorer, confirm the file time did not change.

## Who Benefits

ImageGlass fits a quick pass over photos you took or scans you were asked to check. It does not fit an overwrite of the originals, a folder you are not allowed to open, or a delete from a card that has no second copy.

---

## Fixes

The picture looks soft: switch to actual size. Explorer shows a new time: you saved. Restore the original if you kept one. Thumbnails are blank: you may be in the wrong folder. A directory is not yours: close it.
